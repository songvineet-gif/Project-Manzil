FAQ

Common questions about mortgages in the UAE.

Can't find your answer here? Reach out and we'll walk you through it directly.

General

What does Project Manzil do?
We're a mortgage advisory service. You tell us what you need once, and we compare offers across UAE banks, negotiate on your behalf, and guide you through to closing — so you don't have to deal with the banks yourself.
How is this different from going directly to a bank?
A single bank can only offer you its own rate. We compare multiple banks at once and negotiate on your behalf, so you see the strongest offer available to you rather than just the first one.

Costs & Fees

Does it cost anything to use your service?
No. Our service is completely free for you.
How do you make money if it's free for me?
We're paid a commission by the bank once your mortgage is successfully completed — never by you. This keeps our advice independent, since we're incentivized to find you the right deal, not a specific bank's deal.
Are there any hidden charges?
No hidden charges from us. Standard bank-side costs (such as processing fees, valuation fees, or mortgage registration fees) may apply depending on the bank and property — we'll make sure these are clear to you upfront before you commit.

Eligibility

Can non-residents get a mortgage in the UAE?
Yes. A number of UAE banks lend to non-residents, typically with a higher down payment requirement than for residents. We'll match you with banks that fit your specific situation.
What's the minimum down payment required?
Down payment requirements are set by UAE Central Bank mortgage regulations and vary by residency status and property value — typically starting from around 20–25% for residents, and somewhat higher for non-residents. We'll confirm the exact figure for your case.
What documents do I need to apply?
Generally: passport/Emirates ID, proof of income (salary certificate or bank statements), and property details. Requirements vary slightly by bank and residency status — we'll send you the exact checklist once we understand your case.

Process & Timeline

How long does the process take?
From application to closing typically takes a few weeks, though it depends on the bank, your documentation, and the property. We give you a clear, updated timeline once we start comparing offers on your behalf.
Can I refinance an existing mortgage?
Yes. We can check whether a better rate is currently available and manage the refinancing process end to end if it makes financial sense for you.
What happens if my application is rejected by a bank?
If one bank declines, we simply move to the next best-fit lender from our comparison. Part of our job is finding the bank most likely to approve your specific profile in the first place.

Rates & Terms

Fixed vs. variable rate — which is better?
It depends on your risk tolerance and how long you plan to hold the property. Fixed rates give payment certainty for a set period; variable rates can be lower initially but move with the market. We'll walk you through the trade-offs for your situation.
Can I pay off my mortgage early?
Most UAE banks allow early settlement, sometimes with an early-settlement fee capped by regulation. We'll flag any such terms clearly when comparing offers.

About Us

Are you a licensed broker?
Yes. Manzil is a licensed, independent mortgage brokerage registered in Dubai, UAE. We're not owned by or exclusively tied to any single bank, so our recommendations are based on what's best for you.
Which banks do you work with?
We compare offers across a range of major UAE banks — see our bank network section above. The exact set of banks we approach depends on which ones best fit your profile.
