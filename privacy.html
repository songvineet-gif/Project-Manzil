Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy is a general template drafted for a UAE-based mortgage advisory business. Please have it reviewed by a qualified UAE lawyer before relying on it for a live, customer-facing site.

Project Manzil ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Manazil, a licensed, independent mortgage brokerage based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you submit our contact/loan-comparison form, or reach us via email, phone, or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Details about the property and financing you're enquiring about (e.g. estimated property value, loan type, timing)
  • Any other information you choose to share with us in your message

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data (such as health or biometric data) through this website.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide mortgage advisory services
  • To compare offers on your behalf with UAE banks and share your details with a bank you choose to proceed with
  • To contact you by phone, email, or WhatsApp about your enquiry
  • To improve this website and our services

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share relevant details with a bank or lender solely for the purpose of progressing a mortgage application you have asked us to pursue on your behalf.

3. How we store your information

Form submissions made through this website are processed and stored by our hosting/forms provider (Netlify) and are accessible to our team. We take reasonable steps to keep this information secure, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

4. Cookies

This website may use minimal cookies or local storage required for basic functionality. We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Data retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services and to comply with applicable UAE laws and regulatory record-keeping obligations for licensed mortgage brokers.

7. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates.
