Manzil Properties ("we", "us", "our") is a licensed, independent brokerage firm based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and our services, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices available to you.

By submitting an enquiry or engaging our services, you confirm that you have read and understood this Policy and consent to the handling of your information as described in it.

1. Information we collect

When you submit our contact or loan-comparison form, or reach us by email, telephone, or WhatsApp, we may collect:

  • Your name, telephone number, and email address
  • Details of the property and financing you are enquiring about, such as estimated property value, deposit, loan type, and timing
  • Information relevant to assessing a mortgage application, which may include employment, income, existing financial commitments, residency and identification details
  • Any other information you choose to provide in your message or during our discussions

Some of this information is financial in nature. You provide it voluntarily, and you may decline to provide it — but we may then be unable to source mortgage options for you.

2. Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide mortgage advisory services
  • To assess which banks and products may suit your circumstances
  • To prepare and progress a mortgage application on your instruction
  • To meet legal, regulatory, anti-money-laundering, and record-keeping obligations
  • To improve this website and our services

3. Sharing your information with banks

This is the most significant sharing we do, so we set it out plainly. In order to source and progress a mortgage for you, we will share your personal and financial information with banks and financial institutions in the UAE.

  • We share your information with banks only for the purpose of obtaining indicative terms for you, or of progressing an application you have asked us to pursue.
  • Once your information is with a bank, that bank handles it as an independent data controller under its own privacy policy and terms. We do not control how a bank uses, stores, or retains your information, and we are not responsible for its practices.
  • We may also share information with valuers, insurers, conveyancers, or authorities where this is necessary to progress your mortgage.

By instructing us, you consent to this sharing. If you do not wish us to approach a particular bank, tell us and we will not.

4. We do not sell your information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. How we may contact you

By submitting an enquiry you consent to us contacting you by telephone, SMS, WhatsApp, and email in connection with that enquiry and our services. You may ask us to stop contacting you, or withdraw your consent, at any time using the contact details below, and we will act on that request.

6. Service providers and hosting

Form submissions made through this website are processed and stored by our website and forms provider (Netlify) and are accessible to our team. Our providers may store or process information on servers located outside the United Arab Emirates. Where that is the case, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.

7. Accuracy of the information you give us

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you provide to us is accurate, complete, and current, and for telling us promptly if it changes. We rely on the information you provide and are not responsible for consequences arising from information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold. However, no method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You send information to us at your own risk, and we are not liable for unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss occurring despite reasonable measures, or occurring at a third party such as a bank.

9. How long we keep it

We retain enquiry and client information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, to deal with any subsequent query or complaint, and to comply with applicable UAE laws and regulatory record-keeping obligations applying to licensed brokers. Where information is no longer required, we take steps to delete or anonymise it.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable UAE law, you may ask us to access, correct, update, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it for a particular purpose. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe. Please note that we may need to retain certain information to meet legal or regulatory obligations, or where it is required in connection with a legal claim.

11. Cookies

This website uses only minimal cookies or local storage required for basic functionality. We do not currently use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings.

12. Children

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The version published on this website at the time you use it applies, and the date at the top shows when it was last revised. Please review it periodically.
